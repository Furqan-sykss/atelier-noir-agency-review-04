# Creative Direction Review — Revision 01

The first pass was intentionally functional, but several patterns were too familiar: percentage preloader, oversized centered-ish hero treatment, repeated project-card logic, uniform section rhythm, and a slightly "agency template" service accordion.

Revision principles:
- Remove decorative effects that don't carry narrative meaning.
- Make the hero an editorial composition: text and image occupy different visual territories.
- Give each selected project a different geometry and reading order.
- Vary section density instead of repeating identical padding.
- Treat metadata, captions and rules as part of the identity system.
- Keep motion concentrated around entrance, image reveal and the hero; interaction elsewhere stays quiet.
- Keep the palette warm, black, paper and one muted vermilion accent.
- Mobile gets intentional image/text overlap and smaller editorial metadata rather than a simple desktop stack.

Known placeholder content remains centralized in `data/projects.ts` and obvious `[AGENCY NAME]` tokens.
